Three hex nuts in a stack — back nuts in steel (brand-5), front nut in the mark accent (brand-7). Never flatten it to one colour.

Typography

Barlow Condensed for headings, the wordmark and labels; Barlow for body copy.

Stacknuts

Magento 2 · Free & Open Source

Magento Modules,
Bolted On Tight.

StackNuts is a small collection of open source Magento 2 modules, built and maintained by an Adobe Certified Professional. Body copy runs 15px at 1.55 line height — Barlow, not condensed, set for reading rather than display.
